EasyGo Support

Get help with your EasyGo account, learning progress, community safety, or app access.

Contact us

Email contact@coineasy.xyz.

Never email a password, login code, private key, recovery phrase, or full access token. EasyGo support will not ask for them.

Report or block

  1. Open the menu on the relevant community post.
  2. Choose Report to send the post to EasyGo moderation, or Block to separate that account from your signed-in EasyGo social experience. The block follows your EasyGo account, removes existing follows in both directions, and prevents new follows, likes, and replies between the accounts. Public signed-out views may still show public content.
  3. In EasyGo Settings, open Blocked EasyGo accounts to manage the list. Choose Unblock for one account at a time; unblocking does not restore previous follows.
