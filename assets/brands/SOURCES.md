Base uses the current blue Square (not a system emoji or an approximation).
The component gives it a white field and breathing space; Ethereum's black
diamond also retains a white field in dark panes. Circle's original token
asset replaces the previous generic dollar-sign circle.

EasyGo wallet/explorer headers reuse the repository-owned
`../logo_easygo_ios_1024.png`. We do not replace those headers with a third-party
wallet or BaseScan logo because those are not the products running here.

Asset provenance is not a legal clearance. Before public distribution, review
the then-current [Circle brand use policy](https://6778953.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/6778953/CircleBrandUsePolicy.pdf)
and the linked brand guidance for the actual intended use.
